Kavach

When agents pass work to each other, nobody remembers where a detail came from. Kavach keeps a graph of what every agent was told, read and sent, and traces each detail of an action back to its source before the action runs.

Three scripted agents: a coordinator delegates, a researcher reads, an executor acts. They follow the AgentDojo benchmark's reference solutions, and under attack the coordinator obeys the hidden instruction. No language model is involved. Tools really run, and the outcome is the benchmark's own check.
