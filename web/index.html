How the decision is made

Each component has one clear responsibility.

◇
VAULT · Secret exposure

Looks for credentials and sensitive values in proposed arguments.

⌘
EXECUTOR · Dangerous execution

Checks risky shell and code-execution patterns.

↗
CHANNEL · Data leaving its boundary

Looks for suspicious sensitive-read and external-send relationships across a session.

◎
NAVIGATOR · Policy and context

Uses Invariant Guardrails policies to reason over the current call and available evidence.

Decision meanings

Kavach fails closed when a minister cannot safely decide.

✓
ALLOW

No minister raised a blocking or escalation condition. This is a recommendation, not execution permission by itself.

×
BLOCK

At least one minister found a disallowed or dangerous condition.

!
ESCALATE

Ask a human to review when the available evidence is incomplete or risky.

Research MVP, not a production security boundary. Verify the outcome at the real executor before allowing an agent action.
